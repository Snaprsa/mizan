## Fixed findings

1. **[P1] Stale sales results could be labelled current after comparable evidence changed.** The old reopening logic checked method and property revision but marked the *new* evidence signature as processed while displaying the *old* run. Saved runs are now compared against their actual input/evidence snapshots, calculation version and search configuration. Changed sales evidence triggers recalculation; unchanged runs do not duplicate, and unrelated comparable updates do not repeat income calculations. See [saved-run comparison](Projects/Muqayyim-Downloads/frontend/src/lib/valuation-state.ts:31) and its integration in [the workspace](Projects/Muqayyim-Downloads/frontend/src/components/valuation-workflow.tsx:28).
2. **[P1] Unknown district could borrow an unrelated district benchmark.** When the subject district was empty, the old filter admitted any district in the same city. The new regression initially reproduced a wrong 4% district cap rate in place of a 7% city-wide observation. District matching is now explicit: an unknown district uses city-wide observations only; a known district uses that same district. Rates are never guessed from another district. See [geographic selection](Projects/Muqayyim-Downloads/api/app/services/analysis_workflow.py:99).
3. **[P2] Refresh did not populate newly available blank inputs.** The response updated readiness/evidence but left the editable inputs unchanged. Refresh now fills untouched blank fields from new evidence and preserves unsaved edits. See [refresh handling](Projects/Muqayyim-Downloads/frontend/src/components/valuation-workflow.tsx:98).
