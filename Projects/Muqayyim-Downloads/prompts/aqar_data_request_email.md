## Tips for Success

1. **Send to**: sales@aqar.fm (partnerships team)
2. **Subject Line**: Clear, professional, mentions "partnership" or "data integration"
3. **Timing**: Send during business hours (Sunday-Thursday, 9 AM-4 PM Saudi time)
4. **Follow-up**: If no response within 2 weeks, send gentle follow-up
5. **Relationship Building**: Mention mutual benefits and long-term partnership potential
6. **Compliance**: Clearly state that data will be used professionally and not republished
7. **Documentation**: Attach any relevant credentials, certifications, or company overview documents
